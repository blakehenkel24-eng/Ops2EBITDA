# Investment Thesis Stress Tester

## Purpose

Use this skill to pressure-test a private equity investment thesis or value creation story.

The goal is to identify what must be true, where evidence is weak, and what could break the thesis.

## When to Use

Use this skill for:

- Deal thesis review
- IC memo critique
- Diligence synthesis
- Value creation plan pressure test
- Market thesis review
- Portco strategy review
- Add-on acquisition rationale

## Inputs Needed

Ask for or infer:

- Thesis statement
- Deal rationale
- Value creation assumptions
- Financial targets
- Market assumptions
- Management claims
- Supporting evidence
- Known risks

## Output Structure

### Thesis Summary

Restate the thesis in plain English.

### What Must Be True

| Assumption | Why It Matters | Evidence Needed | Current Evidence | Risk Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Break Points

List what could break the thesis.

### Diligence Questions

List questions to validate or disprove assumptions.

### Post-Close Risks

List execution risks if the deal closes.

## Stress Test Rules

- Do not assume the thesis is correct.
- Separate assumptions from evidence.
- Identify key sensitivities.
- Flag circular logic.
- Identify management dependency.
- Identify external market dependency.
- Identify execution complexity.

## Example Prompt

Use the Investment Thesis Stress Tester skill. Stress-test this investment thesis and identify what must be true, weak evidence, break points, diligence questions, and post-close risks:

[paste thesis]

## Quality Checklist

- Are assumptions explicit?
- Is evidence separated from belief?
- Are break points specific?
- Are diligence questions actionable?
- Are execution risks identified?
