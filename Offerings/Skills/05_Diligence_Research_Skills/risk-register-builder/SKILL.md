# Risk Register Builder

## Purpose

Use this skill to convert diligence findings, operating issues, or workstream concerns into a structured risk register.

## When to Use

Use this skill for:

- Diligence risk tracking
- Value creation planning
- Integration planning
- Board prep
- PMO management
- Exit readiness
- IT / data / cyber assessments
- Operational turnaround work

## Inputs Needed

Ask for or infer:

- Findings or issues
- Context
- Evidence
- Potential impact
- Existing mitigations
- Owner
- Timeline
- Decisions needed

## Output Structure

| Risk | Evidence / Signal | Severity | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner | Timing | Open Question |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Severity Guidance

- **High:** Material impact to EBITDA, cash, growth, deal close, customer retention, or regulatory/compliance posture.
- **Medium:** Meaningful issue requiring management action but not immediately thesis-breaking.
- **Low:** Monitor, but unlikely to materially affect value without other issues.

## Risk Rules

- State risk clearly.
- Do not confuse issue with implication.
- Include evidence.
- Include mitigation.
- Include owner where possible.
- Flag risks without clear mitigation.

## Example Prompt

Use the Risk Register Builder skill. Convert these diligence findings into a risk register with evidence, severity, likelihood, impact, mitigation, owner, and open questions:

[paste findings]

## Quality Checklist

- Are risks specific?
- Is evidence included?
- Are severity and likelihood justified?
- Are mitigations practical?
- Are owners assigned?
- Are open questions visible?
