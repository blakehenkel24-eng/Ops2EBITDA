# Competitor Profile Builder

## Purpose

Use this skill to create competitor profiles that are useful for PE operating decisions.

A good competitor profile should answer:

- Who are they?
- Where do they compete?
- Why do they win?
- Where are they weak?
- What should the portco do about it?

## When to Use

Use this skill for:

- Market intelligence
- Commercial diligence
- Sales enablement
- Pricing strategy
- Product strategy
- Market entry
- Board updates
- Competitor monitoring

## Inputs Needed

Ask for or infer:

- Competitor name(s)
- Market / segment
- Portco context
- Known facts
- Source material
- Desired comparison dimensions

## Output Structure

| Competitor | Positioning | Strengths | Weaknesses | Threat Level | Portco Implication | Recommended Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Individual Competitor Profile

Include:

- Overview
- Target customers
- Offer / product
- Pricing / packaging signals
- Go-to-market motion
- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Implications
- Recommended response

## Rules

- Avoid generic “strong brand” unless supported.
- Translate facts into implications.
- Identify where competitor pressure may affect KPIs.
- Flag confidence and source limitations.
- Recommend action only when warranted.

## Example Prompt

Use the Competitor Profile Builder skill. Build profiles for these competitors and translate findings into pricing, sales, and product implications for the portco:

[paste competitors / notes]

## Quality Checklist

- Does the profile say why the competitor matters?
- Are implications specific?
- Are actions practical?
- Are uncertainties caveated?
- Is threat level justified?
