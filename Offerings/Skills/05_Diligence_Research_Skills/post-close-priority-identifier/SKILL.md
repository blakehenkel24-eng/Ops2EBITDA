# Post-Close Priority Identifier

## Purpose

Use this skill to convert diligence findings into practical post-close priorities.

The goal is to bridge diligence to execution.

## When to Use

Use this skill for:

- Post-close planning
- 100-day planning
- Deal handoff
- VCP creation
- Management onboarding
- Integration planning
- Operating partner transition

## Inputs Needed

Ask for or infer:

- Diligence findings
- Investment thesis
- Key risks
- Value creation opportunities
- Management capacity
- Available data
- Deal timing
- Board expectations

## Output Structure

### Priority Roadmap

| Timing | Priority | Why It Matters | First Action | Owner | KPI / Proof Point |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use timing buckets:

- Day 1
- First 30 days
- Days 31-60
- Days 61-100
- Longer-term

### Immediate Data Requests

List data needed in the first 2 weeks.

### Risks to Stabilize

List risks that need immediate attention.

## Priority Rules

- Day 1 priorities should focus on stabilization and visibility.
- First 30 days should establish baselines and owners.
- Do not overload management.
- Separate quick wins from structural changes.
- Connect priorities to investment thesis.

## Example Prompt

Use the Post-Close Priority Identifier skill. Convert these diligence findings into Day-1, Day-30, Day-60, and Day-100 priorities with owners, KPIs, and proof points.

## Quality Checklist

- Are priorities sequenced realistically?
- Is Day 1 focused on stabilization?
- Are owners and KPIs included?
- Are risks separated from opportunities?
- Is the investment thesis reflected?
