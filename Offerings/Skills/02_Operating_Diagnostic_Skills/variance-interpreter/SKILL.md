# Variance Interpreter

## Purpose

Use this skill to interpret performance variances and explain what they may mean operationally.

The goal is not just to say that a metric is up or down. The goal is to explain likely drivers, implications, and next steps.

## When to Use

Use this skill for:

- Budget vs. actual variance
- Forecast vs. actual variance
- Period-over-period change
- Segment variance
- Site variance
- Customer / SKU variance
- Rep / region variance
- Initiative benefit variance

## Inputs Needed

Ask for or infer:

- Metric
- Actual
- Budget / forecast / prior period
- Variance amount and percentage
- Time period
- Segment
- Management explanation
- Related KPI movement

## Output Structure

### Variance Summary

State the variance plainly.

### Interpretation Table

| Variance | Possible Driver | Operating Implication | Data Needed | Recommended Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Management Questions

List targeted questions.

### Executive Language

Provide concise wording for a board or management update.

## Interpretation Rules

- Separate mathematical variance from operational cause.
- Do not accept management explanation without validation.
- Identify mix, price, volume, cost, timing, and data quality effects.
- Identify whether variance is one-time or structural.
- State what analysis should happen next.

## Example Prompt

Use the Variance Interpreter skill. Revenue is 6% below plan, gross margin is 120 bps above plan, and bookings are flat. Interpret the variance and list data needed.

## Quality Checklist

- Is the variance stated clearly?
- Are possible drivers specific?
- Is timing vs. structural impact considered?
- Are validation steps included?
- Is executive language concise?
