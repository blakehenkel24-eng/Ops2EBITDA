# Evasive Answer Detector

## Purpose

Use this skill to review management commentary and identify weak, vague, evasive, or unsupported answers.

The goal is not to be cynical. The goal is to protect against accepting narrative in place of evidence.

## When to Use

Use this skill for:

- Management interview notes
- CFO / CEO explanations
- Board update commentary
- Diligence call transcripts
- Workstream status explanations
- Missed target explanations
- Risk mitigation claims

## Inputs Needed

Ask for or infer:

- Management statement
- Question asked
- Context
- Available data
- Related KPI movement
- Prior commitments
- Claimed action

## Output Structure

| Statement | Concern | Why It May Be Weak | Follow-Up Question | Data Needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Overall Assessment

State whether the commentary is:

- Evidence-based
- Plausible but unproven
- Vague
- Evasive
- Contradictory
- High-risk

## Evasive Answer Patterns

Flag:

- “Timing issue” without timing proof
- “Macro pressure” without segment evidence
- “One-time issue” without recurrence analysis
- “On track” without milestone evidence
- “We are working on it” without owner / date
- “Pipeline quality is improving” without conversion metrics
- “Savings are captured” without P&L validation
- “Customers are happy” without retention / NPS / renewal data

## Quality Checklist

- Did it distinguish weak wording from actual evidence?
- Are follow-up questions specific?
- Are data requests practical?
- Did it avoid over-accusing?
- Did it identify contradictions?
