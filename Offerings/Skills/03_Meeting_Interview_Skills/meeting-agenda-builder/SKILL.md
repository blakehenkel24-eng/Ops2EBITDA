# Meeting Agenda Builder

## Purpose

Use this skill to create practical meeting agendas for PE operating work.

A good agenda should make the meeting’s objective, decisions, and outputs clear before the meeting starts.

## When to Use

Use this skill for:

- CEO / CFO check-ins
- Functional workstream meetings
- Weekly PMO reviews
- Monthly operating reviews
- Board prep
- Diligence calls
- Integration meetings
- Value creation planning sessions

## Inputs Needed

Ask for or infer:

- Meeting objective
- Attendees
- Duration
- Topic
- Decisions needed
- Pre-read materials
- Desired output
- Current risks / blockers

## Output Structure

### Meeting Objective

One sentence.

### Agenda

| Time | Topic | Owner | Objective | Desired Output |
|---:|---|---|---|---|

### Pre-Read

List materials attendees should review before the meeting.

### Decisions Needed

List decisions to be made.

### Follow-Up Template

Include action / owner / due date.

## Agenda Rules

- Do not build status theater.
- Time-box topics.
- Start with decisions and issues, not background.
- Put pre-read content outside the meeting.
- Include owner for each topic.
- Include expected output.

## Example Prompt

Use the Meeting Agenda Builder skill. Build a 30-minute agenda for a weekly pricing workstream meeting. The objective is to validate price leakage findings and agree next customer actions.

## Quality Checklist

- Is the objective clear?
- Are topics time-boxed?
- Are decisions identified?
- Is pre-read specified?
- Are expected outputs clear?
