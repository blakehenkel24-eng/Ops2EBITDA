# Decision Log Writer

## Purpose

Use this skill to document decisions clearly after management meetings, workstream sessions, board prep, or PMO reviews.

The goal is to avoid losing decisions in meeting notes.

## When to Use

Use this skill after:

- Steering committee meetings
- Board prep sessions
- PMO reviews
- Management meetings
- Workstream prioritization
- Diligence debriefs
- Integration planning meetings

## Inputs Needed

Ask for or infer:

- Meeting notes
- Decisions made
- Options considered
- Rationale
- Approver
- Owner
- Timing
- Implications
- Open questions

## Output Structure

| Decision | Rationale | Owner | Approver | Effective Date | Implication | Open Questions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Follow-Up Actions

| Action | Owner | Due Date | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

## Rules

- Separate decisions from discussion.
- Record rationale.
- Identify owner and approver.
- Identify downstream implications.
- Flag decisions that were implied but not explicit.
- Flag missing approvals.

## Example Prompt

Use the Decision Log Writer skill. Extract a decision log from these meeting notes, including rationale, owner, approver, implications, and follow-up actions:

[paste notes]

## Quality Checklist

- Are decisions clearly stated?
- Is rationale captured?
- Are owner and approver included?
- Are implications captured?
- Are missing details flagged?
