# Evidence Checker

## Purpose

Use this skill to review whether claims are supported by evidence and appropriately caveated.

This is especially important in PE work where unsupported claims can weaken credibility.

## When to Use

Use this skill for:

- Board materials
- Diligence memos
- Value creation plans
- KPI analysis
- Market research
- Investment thesis writing
- Exit readiness narratives
- Client deliverables

## Inputs Needed

Ask for or infer:

- Draft content
- Source material
- Available data
- Claims to check
- Audience
- Required rigor

## Output Structure

| Claim | Evidence Provided | Evidence Gap | Risk of Overstatement | Recommended Rewrite |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Claims Needing Support

List claims that require additional data or source support.

### Safer Language

Provide revised claims with appropriate caveats.

## Evidence Rules

Flag:

- Causal claims without proof
- Quantified claims without source
- Market claims without evidence
- “Best-in-class” claims
- “Significant” without magnitude
- EBITDA impact without calculation
- “Customers prefer” without customer evidence
- “One-time issue” without recurrence analysis

## Example Prompt

Use the Evidence Checker skill. Review this board update for unsupported claims, missing evidence, and overstatement risk. Suggest safer rewrites:

[paste content]

## Quality Checklist

- Are unsupported claims identified?
- Are safer rewrites provided?
- Are evidence gaps specific?
- Are assumptions visible?
- Is overstatement risk reduced?
