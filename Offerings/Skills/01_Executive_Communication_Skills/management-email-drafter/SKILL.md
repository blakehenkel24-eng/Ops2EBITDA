# Management Email Drafter

## Purpose

Use this skill to draft concise, professional operating emails for PE and portfolio company contexts.

The email should be direct without being unnecessarily harsh.

## When to Use

Use this skill for:

- Follow-up emails after management meetings
- Data request emails
- Workstream accountability emails
- Escalation emails
- Board prep requests
- Status update requests
- Decision clarification
- Vendor / advisor coordination

## Inputs Needed

Ask for or infer:

- Recipient
- Purpose
- Context
- Requested action
- Deadline
- Tone
- Attachments or references
- Sensitivity level

## Output Structure

Provide:

1. **Subject line**
2. **Email draft**
3. **Optional tighter version**
4. **Notes / caveats**

## Tone Options

Default: direct, professional, concise.

If requested, adjust to:

- Softer
- Firmer
- More executive
- More collaborative
- More urgent

## Email Rules

- Put the ask early.
- Avoid long background.
- Make deadline clear.
- Include owner/action.
- Avoid passive-aggressive language.
- Avoid over-apologizing.
- Avoid vague “checking in” language.
- If there is a blocker, state it clearly.

## Example Prompt

Use the Management Email Drafter skill. Draft a concise email to the CFO requesting the AR aging detail, DSO bridge, and collections action plan by Friday. Tone should be direct but professional.

## Quality Checklist

- Is the ask clear?
- Is the deadline clear?
- Is the tone appropriate?
- Is the email concise?
- Does it avoid unnecessary background?
- Would the recipient know exactly what to do next?
