# Executive Summary Builder

## Purpose

Use this skill to produce crisp executive summaries from messy notes, analysis, meeting transcripts, dashboard commentary, or workstream materials.

The output should quickly tell a senior reader:

- What matters
- Why it matters
- What should happen next
- What risks or decisions require attention

## When to Use

Use this skill for:

- One-page memos
- Board pre-reads
- Management updates
- Operating review summaries
- Diligence summaries
- Workstream recap notes
- Executive emails
- Partner-ready updates

## Inputs Needed

Useful inputs include:

- Raw notes or draft
- Audience
- Purpose
- Desired length
- Required decision
- KPI or financial data
- Context on company / workstream
- Known risks or caveats

If audience is not specified, assume a PE operating partner or senior executive.

## Output Structure

### Executive Summary

Use 4-7 bullets maximum.

Each bullet should include:

- Finding / update
- Implication
- Action or decision, where relevant

### Key Implications

| Implication | Why It Matters | Action / Decision |
|---|---|---|

### Open Items

List missing facts, required data, or unresolved decisions.

## Writing Rules

- Put the main point first.
- Remove background unless it changes the decision.
- Avoid phrases like “it is important to note.”
- Avoid unsupported adjectives.
- Keep bullets parallel.
- Make the action clear.
- Use active voice.
- Prefer short sentences.

## Summary Types

### Decision Summary

Use when the reader needs to decide.

Include:
- Decision required
- Options
- Recommendation
- Rationale
- Risk / caveat

### Update Summary

Use when the reader needs status.

Include:
- What changed
- Status
- Impact
- Next step

### Diagnostic Summary

Use when the reader needs to understand an issue.

Include:
- Symptom
- Likely driver
- Data needed
- Action path

## Quality Checklist

- Is the first bullet the most important?
- Could a senior reader understand the issue in under 60 seconds?
- Does every bullet have a reason to exist?
- Are implications explicit?
- Are actions clear?
- Are caveats visible?
