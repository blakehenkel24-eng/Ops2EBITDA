# Slide Bullet Sharpener

## Purpose

Use this skill to turn rough notes into clean, concise slide bullets for consulting-style and PE operating materials.

This skill is for content quality, not visual design.

## When to Use

Use this skill for:

- Board deck bullets
- Workstream update slides
- Diligence slides
- Executive summary slides
- KPI commentary
- Initiative status slides
- Operating review slides

## Inputs Needed

Ask for or infer:

- Slide title / intended takeaway
- Audience
- Rough bullets or notes
- Desired tone
- Data points to preserve
- Whether bullets should be action-oriented or diagnostic

## Output Structure

### Revised Slide Takeaway

One sentence that could serve as the slide headline.

### Revised Bullets

3-5 bullets maximum unless the user asks for more.

### Notes on Changes

Briefly explain what was tightened or clarified.

### Remaining Gaps

Flag missing data or unsupported claims.

## Bullet Rules

Good bullets:

- Lead with the implication
- Include evidence where available
- Are specific
- Use parallel structure
- Are short enough for a slide
- Avoid generic verbs

Bad bullets:

- “Continue to monitor…”
- “Leverage opportunities…”
- “Drive alignment…”
- “Optimize synergies…”
- “Improve performance…” without mechanism
- Long multi-clause paragraphs disguised as bullets

## Before / After Pattern

Weak:
- Sales productivity has been challenging due to various pipeline issues.

Better:
- Pipeline coverage fell below 2.5x target, increasing Q3 bookings risk unless late-stage conversion improves.

## Quality Checklist

- Does the slide have a clear takeaway?
- Are bullets specific and non-overlapping?
- Is each bullet evidence-based?
- Is the language concise?
- Are actions or implications clear?
- Would this survive partner review?
