# Ops2EBITDA ICP Profiles

## Source Basis

This ICP profile set is based on the current Ops2EBITDA product positioning: a serious, consulting-grade private equity operations knowledge base focused on value creation logic, operating-partner playbooks, industry intelligence, KPI interpretation, and EBITDA-oriented execution.

Ops2EBITDA should not be positioned as a beginner course, generic chatbot, or generic SaaS dashboard. The strongest positioning is as an operator-grade reference system for business-fluent professionals who need to connect operating actions to KPIs, EBITDA, cash flow, risk, and enterprise value.

---

## Product Summary

**Product name:** Ops2EBITDA  
**Category:** Private equity operations knowledge base  
**Core promise:** Help PE professionals, consultants, and acquisition-oriented operators understand how portfolio company improvement translates into EBITDA, cash flow, and enterprise value.  
**Primary content areas:**

- Value creation playbooks
- Industry intelligence
- KPI libraries
- Operating diagnostics
- 100-day plan logic
- Project playbooks
- Process diagrams
- Common mistakes and execution failure modes
- EBITDA, cash flow, and enterprise value linkage

---

## Positioning Statement

**Ops2EBITDA is an operating knowledge base for PE professionals, consultants, and acquisition entrepreneurs who need to understand how portfolio company improvement actually turns into EBITDA, cash flow, and enterprise value.**

# ICP 1: PE Consultant / Due Diligence Consultant

## Profile

**Title examples:**

- Associate Consultant
- Consultant
- Senior Consultant
- Engagement Manager
- PE Value Creation Consultant
- Commercial Diligence Consultant
- IT Diligence Consultant
- Operations Consultant

**Company type:**

- Consulting firm serving PE clients
- Boutique diligence firm
- Strategy consulting firm
- Value creation advisory firm
- Technology diligence firm

**Seniority:** Analyst through Manager

## Core Job-to-be-Done

Produce sharper diligence findings, value creation hypotheses, operating assessments, and client-ready deliverables faster.

## Why They Care

This user needs reusable knowledge that helps them move from generic findings to PE-specific implications. They are often asked to answer:

- What does this operational issue mean for EBITDA?
- What should management do in the first 100 days?
- What KPIs should be requested?
- What value creation initiatives are credible in this industry?
- What risks should a sponsor underwrite?

## Pain Points

- Too much time spent rebuilding basic playbook logic
- Needs sharper language for client deliverables
- Wants to avoid shallow consultant-speak
- Often lacks industry-specific operating benchmarks or diagnostic structures
- Needs to translate findings into board-level value creation narratives

## Best Product Hook

**A reference system for turning diligence observations into operating implications.**

## Messaging Angle

Move faster from findings to value creation logic. Use structured briefings, KPI interpretation, and operating playbooks built for PE-facing work.

## Features That Matter Most

- Industry-specific playbooks
- Common mistake patterns
- KPI interpretation
- 100-day plan structures
- Process diagrams
- EBITDA bridge logic
- Consulting-grade writing tone

## Buying Likelihood

**High** for individual users and small consulting teams.

## Why This ICP Is Strong

This is the closest fit to the current product. The user already has the business fluency needed to appreciate the depth, and the product directly supports real work: diligence, value creation planning, deliverables, and client conversations.

# ICP 2: Emerging PE Ops Professional

## Profile

**Title examples:**

- PE Associate
- Senior Associate
- VP
- Portfolio Operations Associate
- Value Creation Associate
- Portfolio Support Analyst

**Company type:**

- Private equity fund
- Growth equity fund
- Lower-middle-market sponsor
- Independent sponsor platform

**Seniority:** Early-to-mid career

## Core Job-to-be-Done

Build operating-partner-level fluency fast enough to be useful in diligence, value creation planning, board prep, and portfolio company conversations.

## Why They Care

This user understands finance and strategy but does not yet have deep pattern recognition across operations. They want to know:

- What levers actually move EBITDA?
- Which KPIs matter by industry?
- What does good look like in pricing, sales, procurement, working capital, labor productivity, and tech enablement?
- How do operating partners diagnose issues and convert them into action plans?

## Pain Points

- Knows the language of PE but not enough operating detail
- Needs to sound credible with operating partners, CEOs, and functional leaders
- Learns from scattered PDFs, old decks, podcasts, and fragmented notes
- Struggles to connect operational actions to EBITDA, cash flow, risk, and enterprise value

## Best Product Hook

**Build operating-partner judgment without waiting ten years to see every playbook firsthand.**

## Messaging Angle

Learn the operating logic behind PE value creation: diagnostics, KPIs, levers, mistakes, and 100-day execution patterns.

## Features That Matter Most

- Value creation playbooks
- KPI library
- Industry profiles
- Diligence-to-100-day-plan logic
- Cross-links between actions, KPIs, EBITDA, cash flow, and enterprise value

## Buying Likelihood

**Medium to high**, especially if priced affordably for individual professionals.

## Why This ICP Is Strong

This user has high motivation and a clear career ROI. They are likely to use the product repeatedly because the content helps them build professional judgment, not just complete one task.

# ICP 3: Independent Sponsor / Search Fund Operator

## Profile

**Title examples:**

- Independent Sponsor
- Search Fund Entrepreneur
- Acquisition Entrepreneur
- Self-Funded Searcher
- CEO-in-Waiting
- ETA Investor

**Company type:**

- Independent sponsor
- Traditional search fund
- Self-funded search
- Entrepreneurship-through-acquisition platform
- Small acquisition vehicle

**Seniority:** Founder/operator

## Core Job-to-be-Done

Understand how to evaluate, acquire, and improve a lower-middle-market business without having a full PE operating team.

## Why They Care

This user needs practical operator knowledge. They are not trying to become academics. They want to know:

- What should be evaluated before buying a business?
- Which operational gaps are fixable?
- Which KPIs reveal quality of earnings or execution risk?
- What should happen in the first 100 days?
- How do pricing, sales, procurement, working capital, and labor productivity translate to cash flow?

## Pain Points

- Lacks institutional PE resources
- Has to be investor, operator, analyst, and strategist at once
- Needs practical frameworks without paying consultants for every question
- Wants to avoid buying a business with hidden operational issues
- Needs confidence before speaking with lenders, sellers, and investors

## Best Product Hook

**The PE operating playbook for buyers without a full portfolio operations team.**

## Messaging Angle

Learn how experienced PE operators assess value creation, risk, KPIs, and post-close execution before and after an acquisition.

## Features That Matter Most

- Acquisition diligence checklists
- 100-day planning
- KPI interpretation
- Working capital and margin improvement playbooks
- Industry profiles
- Common operational red flags

## Buying Likelihood

**High** if positioned as a practical acquisition/operator reference.

## Why This ICP Is Strong

This audience has an obvious pain: they need PE-grade operating knowledge but usually lack institutional resources. The product can become a practical companion for diligence and post-close execution.

# ICP 5: Operating Partner / Value Creation Leader

## Profile

**Title examples:**

- Operating Partner
- Portfolio Operations Partner
- Head of Value Creation
- VP Portfolio Operations
- Operating Executive
- Portfolio Transformation Lead

**Company type:**

- Private equity firm
- Growth equity firm
- Platform investment group
- Family office with direct control investments

**Seniority:** Senior

## Core Job-to-be-Done

Codify operating knowledge, standardize playbooks, and create a shared language across deal teams, junior ops staff, consultants, and portfolio leadership.

## Why They Care

This user already knows much of the material. They do not need a beginner education product. They may care if Ops2EBITDA helps:

- Train junior team members
- Standardize value creation thinking
- Reduce repeated explanations
- Create a common operating vocabulary
- Support internal playbook development

## Pain Points

- Junior professionals lack operating pattern recognition
- Deal teams overstate value creation opportunities without execution detail
- Portfolio company teams misunderstand PE priorities
- Internal knowledge is trapped in decks, memories, and partner experience
- Hard to scale operating partner judgment across the firm

## Best Product Hook

**A shared operating reference system for scaling value creation judgment across the firm.**

## Messaging Angle

Codify the operating logic behind diagnostics, KPIs, levers, 100-day plans, and common execution failures.

## Features That Matter Most

- Serious editorial tone
- Advanced playbooks
- Industry-specific operating logic
- Cross-referenced KPI and EBITDA impact
- Ability to support internal training
- No gamified learning feel

## Buying Likelihood

**Medium** as a direct buyer; **high** as an influencer if product quality is strong.

This audience is valuable but harder to impress. They may not need the product personally unless it clearly helps scale their expertise across teams. The product must avoid sounding introductory or academic for this segment.

# Recommended Initial Targeting Strategy

## Primary Wedge

The cleanest initial wedge is:

**PE consultants and emerging PE operations professionals.**

These users are business-fluent, actively motivated, and need the product to perform better in real work. They are close enough to the problem to value dense, practical content, but not so senior that they already have every pattern internalized.

## Secondary Wedge

The best secondary commercial wedge is:

**Independent sponsors and search fund operators.**

This audience has a strong need for PE-grade operating knowledge but often lacks access to institutional resources. The product can be positioned as a practical reference for evaluating, acquiring, and improving lower-middle-market businesses.

## B2B Expansion Path

The best team-level expansion path is:

**Boutique PE advisory firms.**

This segment could justify team pricing if the product becomes useful for analyst training, proposal development, diligence support, and value creation delivery.

Ops2EBITDA should be positioned first as a **professional operating reference system**, not as an education app.

The strongest initial ICPs are:

1. **PE consultants and diligence consultants**
2. **Emerging PE operations professionals**
3. **Independent sponsors and search fund operators**

The strongest website segmentation is:

- **For Advisors**
- **For Investors**
- **For Buyers**

The product should keep its tone serious, editorial, and operator-grade. Do not water down the homepage for beginners. Serve beginner-adjacent users through content marketing, not the core brand promise.
