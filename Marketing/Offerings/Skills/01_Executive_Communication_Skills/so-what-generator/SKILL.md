# So-What Generator

## Purpose

Use this skill to convert observations into implications and actions.

The goal is to prevent passive reporting. Every observation should answer:

**So what? What should we do about it? Why does it matter financially or operationally?**

## When to Use

Use this skill for:

- KPI commentary
- Diligence findings
- Board updates
- Dashboard observations
- Market research findings
- Workstream notes
- Analysis outputs
- Executive summaries

## Inputs Needed

Useful inputs:

- Observation or data point
- Context
- KPI baseline or target
- Relevant business function
- Known management explanation
- Available supporting data

## Output Structure

Use this table:

| Observation | So What | Operating Implication | Recommended Action | KPI / Value Link | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Interpretation Rules

For each observation, identify:

1. What the fact says
2. Why it matters
3. What it may imply operationally
4. What data would validate it
5. What action should be considered
6. Which KPI or value lever is affected

## Confidence Labels

Use:

- **High:** Direct evidence supports the implication.
- **Medium:** Plausible but needs more data.
- **Low:** Early hypothesis only.

## Example

Observation:
Gross margin declined 180 bps quarter-over-quarter.

So what:
Margin pressure may reflect price leakage, mix shift, input cost increases, or discounting. Management needs to isolate the driver by customer, SKU, region, and sales rep before choosing corrective action.

## Quality Checklist

- Did every observation get an implication?
- Did every implication connect to an action?
- Is the KPI or value link explicit?
- Are assumptions labeled?
- Is confidence stated?
- Is the output useful for decision-making?
