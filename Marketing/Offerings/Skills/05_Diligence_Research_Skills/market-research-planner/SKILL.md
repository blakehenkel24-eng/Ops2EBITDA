# Market Research Planner

## Purpose

Use this skill to structure market research for PE operating and diligence work.

The goal is not generic research. The goal is research that informs decisions.

## When to Use

Use this skill for:

- Industry research
- Competitor research
- Customer segment analysis
- Vendor landscape research
- Market trend monitoring
- Diligence workplans
- Portco strategy projects
- New market entry analysis

## Inputs Needed

Ask for or infer:

- Research topic
- Company / industry
- Decision to support
- Time available
- Known hypotheses
- Desired output
- Source constraints

## Output Structure

### Research Objective

Define the decision the research should support.

### Issue Tree

Break the topic into key research questions.

### Research Plan

| Question | Why It Matters | Sources | Method | Output | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Expected Deliverables

List the final artifacts.

### Watchouts

List likely source limitations or bias.

## Research Rules

- Start with the decision.
- Do not collect facts without implications.
- Prioritize source quality.
- Separate market fact from operating implication.
- Identify what should be monitored over time.
- Caveat uncertain or conflicting findings.

## Example Prompt

Use the Market Research Planner skill. Build a research plan for understanding the competitive landscape and pricing dynamics in a vertical SaaS market.

## Quality Checklist

- Is the decision clear?
- Are questions prioritized?
- Are source types specific?
- Are outputs defined?
- Are operating implications expected?
