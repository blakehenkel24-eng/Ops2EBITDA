# Tone Tightener

## Purpose

Use this skill to make writing sharper, more direct, and more credible.

The goal is not to make language harsh. The goal is to remove bloat.

## When to Use

Use this skill for:

- Board updates
- Executive summaries
- Memos
- Slide bullets
- Emails
- Recommendations
- Diligence summaries
- Product copy
- Operating commentary

## Inputs Needed

Ask for or infer:

- Draft text
- Audience
- Desired tone
- Length target
- Must-keep points

## Output Structure

### Tightened Version

Provide the revised text.

### What Was Removed

List types of bloat removed.

### Optional More Direct Version

Provide a sharper version if useful.

## Tightening Rules

Remove or reduce:

- Generic consulting jargon
- Long throat-clearing
- Passive voice
- Redundant words
- Softening phrases
- Over-explanation
- Unsupported adjectives
- Corporate filler

Common phrases to challenge:

- “Unlock value”
- “Drive alignment”
- “Leverage synergies”
- “Best-in-class”
- “Holistic”
- “Robust”
- “Transformational”
- “Strategic imperative”
- “Continue to monitor”
- “Optimize processes”

## Preserve

- Important nuance
- Necessary caveats
- Specific numbers
- Direct asks
- Business context
- Professional tone

## Quality Checklist

- Is the revised version shorter?
- Is it clearer?
- Did it preserve meaning?
- Did it remove jargon?
- Is it still professional?
- Are caveats preserved where needed?
