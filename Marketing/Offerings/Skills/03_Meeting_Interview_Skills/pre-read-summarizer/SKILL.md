# Pre-Read Summarizer

## Purpose

Use this skill to summarize pre-read materials before a meeting.

The output should help the user walk into the meeting knowing what matters, what is unclear, and what to ask.

## When to Use

Use this skill for:

- Board pre-reads
- Monthly operating reviews
- Management updates
- Diligence materials
- KPI dashboards
- Workstream status documents
- Research briefs

## Inputs Needed

Ask for or infer:

- Pre-read material
- Meeting objective
- Audience
- Decisions expected
- Known concerns
- Desired level of detail

## Output Structure

### 60-Second Summary

3-5 bullets.

### What Matters

List the most important points.

### What Is Unclear

List gaps, contradictions, or unsupported claims.

### Questions to Ask

Provide targeted questions.

### Decisions / Actions Needed

List likely decisions or actions.

## Summary Rules

- Do not summarize every section equally.
- Prioritize decision-relevant information.
- Flag weak evidence.
- State implications.
- Keep it concise.

## Quality Checklist

- Is the summary decision-oriented?
- Are unclear points flagged?
- Are questions specific?
- Are implications clear?
- Is it concise enough to use before a meeting?
