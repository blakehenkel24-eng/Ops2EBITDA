# Management Interview Guide Builder

## Purpose

Use this skill to build targeted management interview guides for PE operating work.

The goal is to prepare questions that reveal performance drivers, risks, owner accountability, data quality, and value creation opportunities.

## When to Use

Use this skill for:

- Management diligence calls
- CEO / CFO operating reviews
- Functional leader interviews
- 100-day planning
- Value creation diagnostics
- Add-on integration discovery
- Board prep
- Workstream kickoff meetings

## Inputs Needed

Ask for or infer:

- Interviewee role
- Company context
- Topic / workstream
- Objective of interview
- Known issues
- Available data
- Time available
- Desired output

## Output Structure

### Interview Objective

State the purpose of the conversation.

### Pre-Read Checklist

List what to review beforehand.

### Question Guide

| Topic | Question | Why It Matters | Follow-Up Probe |
|---|---|---|---|

### Red Flags to Watch For

List vague, evasive, or concerning answer patterns.

### Post-Interview Capture Template

Include fields for findings, evidence, open questions, and follow-ups.

## Question Quality Rules

Good questions are:

- Specific
- Open-ended but anchored
- Evidence-seeking
- Role-relevant
- Tied to value levers
- Designed to reveal owner accountability

Avoid:

- Generic “tell me about your function”
- Questions that invite theater
- Multi-part questions that are hard to answer
- Questions answerable from the deck
- Overly aggressive wording unless requested

## Example Prompt

Use the Management Interview Guide Builder skill. Create a 45-minute CFO interview guide for diagnosing working capital deterioration at a PE-backed distributor.

## Quality Checklist

- Does the guide match the interviewee’s role?
- Are questions tied to operating drivers?
- Are follow-up probes included?
- Are red flags identified?
- Is there a capture template?
