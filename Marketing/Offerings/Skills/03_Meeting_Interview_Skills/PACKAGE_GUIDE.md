# Meeting Interview Skills

This package contains uploadable `SKILL.md` files for PE operating workflows.

## Included Skills

- **Management Interview Guide Builder** — Use when preparing structured interview guides for CEO, CFO, CRO, COO, functional leaders, portco management, or diligence calls.
- **Meeting Agenda Builder** — Use when creating crisp meeting agendas with objectives, topics, decisions needed, timing, and expected outputs.
- **Follow-Up Tracker Builder** — Use when converting meeting notes, calls, workstream updates, or diligence conversations into action trackers with owners and due dates.
- **Evasive Answer Detector** — Use when reviewing management responses, interview notes, or update commentary to identify vague, unsupported, evasive, or contradictory answers.
- **Decision Log Writer** — Use when documenting decisions made, rationale, owners, implications, open questions, and next steps from meetings or workstreams.
- **Pre-Read Summarizer** — Use when summarizing meeting pre-reads, board materials, management updates, or reports into what matters, questions, and decisions.
