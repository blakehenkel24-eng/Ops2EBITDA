# Content Enhancement Design - Curated Articles

## Goal
Enhance three curated articles in `content/curated-articles/` by removing citations, increasing volume to 5 sections, and adopting a punchy, expert tone.

## Target Files
1. `working-capital-improvement.json`
2. `sales-productivity-improvement.json`
3. `customer-retention-churn-reduction.json`

## Requirements
- **No Citations:** Remove all mentions of specific authors or books (e.g., Adam Coffey, Peter Howson, Private Equity 4.0).
- **Volume:** Exactly 5 sections per article.
- **Tone:** Senior Operating Partner level—authoritative, direct, and focused on value creation.
- **Format:** Maintain existing JSON structure (`id`, `articleSections` array with `title`, `body`, and optional `callout`).

## Content Strategy

### 1. Working Capital Improvement
- **Section 1: The Hidden Bank Account (CCC)** - Reframe as the primary lever for deleveraging.
- **Section 2: DSO & DPO Optimization** - Focus on aggressive credit policies and vendor leverage.
- **Section 3: Inventory & SKU Discipline** - Moving beyond rationalization to demand-driven supply chains.
- **Section 4: Procurement & Vendor Consolidation** - Centralizing spend to drive payment term standardization.
- **Section 5: Building a Cash Culture** - Management incentives and the weekly cash cadence.

### 2. Sales Productivity Improvement
- **Section 1: The Repeatable Revenue Machine** - Audit-driven performance leveling.
- **Section 2: Incentives & Territory Mastery** - Aligning commissions with EBITDA, not just volume.
- **Section 3: Tech-Enabled Forecasting** - Using CRM data as a predictive capital allocation tool.
- **Section 4: The Sales Playbook** - Hard-coding the sales process to eliminate "hero culture".
- **Section 5: Performance Pipelining** - Continuous talent upgrade and structured onboarding.

### 3. Customer Retention & Churn Reduction
- **Section 1: Valuation Preservation** - Cohort analysis as a defense against multiple compression.
- **Section 2: The Proactive Health Score** - Algorithmic intervention before the cancelation request.
- **Section 3: Structural Stickiness** - Multi-year contracts and deep workflow integration.
- **Section 4: Pricing & Packaging Strategy** - Using tiers and bundling to lock in long-term value.
- **Section 5: The Feedback Flywheel** - Closing the gap between customer success and product engineering.

## Validation
- Verify each file has exactly 5 sections.
- Verify zero citations remain.
- Verify valid JSON structure.
