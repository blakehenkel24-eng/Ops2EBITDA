# Design: Curated Articles High-Depth Standard Update

## Objective
Update 15 curated article JSON files to a "Senior Operating Partner" standard, increasing depth, technical specificity, and financial rigor.

## Standard Requirements
- **Structure:** Exactly 5 sections per article.
- **Depth:** Exactly 3 detailed paragraphs per section.
- **Tone:** Senior Operating Partner (hard-hitting, professional, focus on value creation).
- **Inclusions:**
    - Concrete math (EBITDA impact, basis points, margin expansion, etc.).
    - Specific tools (Software, frameworks, platforms).
- **Exclusions:** No citations.

## Content Template (per section)
1.  **Paragraph 1: Strategic Context.** Defines the operational lever and its importance to the investment thesis. Focuses on the "why" from an investor/operator perspective.
2.  **Paragraph 2: The Tooling & Implementation.** Identifies specific tools (e.g., Salesforce, NetSuite, PowerBI, Lean Six Sigma) and how they are deployed to capture the value.
3.  **Paragraph 3: The EBITDA Math.** Provides a concrete numerical example. Shows the calculation of how the change moves the needle on EBITDA or valuation (e.g., "A 100bps improvement in gross margin on $200M revenue adds $2M to EBITDA, which at a 12x exit multiple, creates $24M in enterprise value").
