## Strengths

- Owns a clear category phrase: “the value creation platform for PE.”
- Connects strategy, initiative execution, metrics, and reporting in one narrative.
- Uses named practitioners and recognizable PE firms to make claims credible.
- Converts content into a platform demo rather than leaving it as isolated thought leadership.

## Weaknesses and Openings for Ops2EBITDA

- Enterprise software is heavier, slower to buy, and less accessible to individuals or small advisory firms.
- Its content explains the operating system; Ops2EBITDA can win the underlying task-level searches with downloadable work products.
- Public pricing is absent, creating room for transparent, self-serve offers.

## Content Lesson

Publish around the full value-creation lifecycle, but make each article resolve into a concrete operating artifact. Use named workflows and real outputs, not generic transformation language.
