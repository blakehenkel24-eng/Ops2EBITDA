## Weaknesses and Openings for Ops2EBITDA

- Breadth creates uneven quality and limited category coherence.
- Marketplace authorship can weaken trust compared with one operator-grade editorial standard.
- Ops2EBITDA can be narrower, PE-native, better integrated, and clearer about how artifacts connect from diligence through execution.

## Content Lesson

Product pages are content. Each Ops2EBITDA asset page should target an exact job-to-be-done query and show inputs, tabs/files, example outputs, preview images, QA logic, and the next related artifact.
