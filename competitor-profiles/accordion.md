## Strengths

- Uses proprietary survey data to create claims competitors must cite or react to.
- Writes for a sharply defined role under pressure, especially the PE-backed CFO.
- Leads with a bottom-line-up-front summary and then turns findings into a playbook.
- Repackages one research asset into multiple articles, industry cuts, and conversion pages.

## Weaknesses and Openings for Ops2EBITDA

- Content is CFO-heavy; operating associates, consultants, independent sponsors, and cross-functional value-creation teams remain less directly served.
- Advice often points to consulting help rather than a self-serve artifact.
- Ops2EBITDA can convert the same class of problem into a model, checklist, skill, or project kit at transparent prices.

## Content Lesson

The highest-confidence pattern in this review is original data turned into a content franchise. Ops2EBITDA should create a recurring “State of PE Value Creation Work” benchmark and derive role-, industry-, and workflow-specific pieces from it.
