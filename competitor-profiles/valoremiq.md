## Weaknesses and Openings for Ops2EBITDA

- Early-stage proof and editorial depth appear limited.
- The platform attacks tracking; Ops2EBITDA can own how to diagnose, structure, and execute each lever before tracking begins.
- The site’s “spreadsheet chaos” framing should not be copied wholesale because Excel is also an Ops2EBITDA product strength.

## Content Lesson
