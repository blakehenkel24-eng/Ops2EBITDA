## What This Product Is

Ops2EBITDA is a private equity operations knowledge platform with two layers:

1. A free knowledge base that teaches how PE value creation works.
2. A paid asset library that helps users do the work.

It is best understood as a serious, consulting-grade PE operations reference plus a store of downloadable operating assets.

## Core Product Thesis

The product exists to:

1. Teach users how private equity operating work actually works.
2. Help them perform that work with better structure, speed, and output quality.
3. Build trust through free content, then monetize through practical execution assets.

## What The Free Product Currently Is

The free layer is a static, curated PE operations knowledge base.

It covers:

- PE fundamentals
- value creation logic
- KPI interpretation
- operating playbooks
- industry intelligence
- cross-linked reasoning from issue to action to KPI to EBITDA, cash flow, and enterprise value

This free layer is the trust-building and educational engine of the business. It is not the paid catalog.

## What The Paid Product Currently Is

The paid layer is a library of downloadable PE operating assets.

The commercial structure is intentionally simple. It is not a SaaS subscription product, a generic course, or a broad services marketplace. Users browse the catalog and buy the specific asset, package, or bundle they need.

## Primary User

The primary user is a business-fluent professional who wants operating-partner-level thinking and higher-quality work product.

Typical users include:

- private equity professionals
- operating partners
- value creation team members
- management consultants
- independent sponsors
- search fund operators

## Experience Principles

- Lead with serious PE operating content, not generic learning-app patterns.
- Keep the free knowledge base and the paid asset catalog clearly separated.
- Position the paid layer as practical execution assets, not vague education products.
- Favor dense, practical, consulting-grade outputs over lightweight templates.
- Preserve a simple commerce model with direct, understandable offers.

## What This Product Is Not

- A generic AI chatbot
- A beginner business learning app
- A SaaS dashboard with complex subscription tiers
- A community product
- A general consulting marketplace
