# PE Ops Knowledge Base

## Register

Product.

## Product Purpose

PE Ops Knowledge Base is a static private equity operations reference for deep operator briefings, value creation logic, industry intelligence, industry project playbooks, and KPI interpretation. The product should feel like a serious consulting-grade knowledge system, not a beginner course, notes app, or generic chatbot.

## Primary User

The primary user is a business professional or consultant with strong business fluency who wants to build operating-partner-level understanding of portfolio company improvement. They prefer dense, practical briefings with clear operating logic, structured examples, visual process diagrams, and direct links between actions, KPIs, EBITDA, cash flow, risk, and enterprise value.

## Experience Principles

- Lead with briefings and operating decisions, not learning modules.
- Favor depth, editorial hierarchy, and serious writing over cards and gamified study affordances.
- Organize navigation around user needs and industries where possible.
- Keep Phase 2 AI/study features clearly reserved, not central to the V1 experience.
- Every interface should support reading, scanning, search, and cross-reference discovery.

## Anti-References

- Generic SaaS dashboard with hero metrics and repeated cards.
- Beginner learning center with definitions, study questions, and key terms.
- Decorative glassmorphism, novelty gradients, or dashboard chrome that competes with the content.
